Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

1. What we collect

When you use our quote or drawing form, we collect your name, phone number, optional email, project details, and any files you attach (photos, sketches, PDFs, CAD files). When you pay online, Razorpay collects your payment details directly.

2. How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote.
  • To deliver the services you have paid for.
  • To keep records of payments and projects.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Who processes it

  • Form delivery: our enquiry form is delivered by FormSubmit, which passes your submission and attachments to our email.
  • Email: Google (Gmail) stores the enquiries we receive.
  • Payments: Razorpay processes online payments under its own privacy policy. We do not store your card, UPI or bank details.
  • Fonts: this site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which can see your IP address when the page loads.

4. Cookies and tracking

This site does not use analytics or advertising cookies. Third-party services listed above may set their own cookies, for example during checkout.

5. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details and project files only as long as needed to deliver the project and meet record-keeping obligations, then delete them on request where the law allows.

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by writing to the email below.
