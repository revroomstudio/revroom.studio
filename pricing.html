Included

  • Plot plan with setbacks marked
  • 1 floor-plan option for each floor, dimensioned, with furniture layout

  • Door and window schedule

Not included

  • Structural design and drawings (engineer-signed, quoted separately)
  • Detailed plumbing and electrical engineering
  • Municipal approval drawings and submission
  • BOQ and cost estimates
  • Site visits and supervision
  • Interior design, landscaping, walkthroughs
  • Changes after sign-off

What we need from you

  • Plot dimensions and shape, road side and north direction
  • Survey sketch or title document
  • Number of floors and rooms needed
  • Budget range and style references
  • Vastu preference, if any
  • Local setback and floor-area rules, if known

Please note: these drawings are not a structural design. Have a licensed structural engineer design and sign the structure before you build.

How payment works

  • Orders under ₹10,000: 100% upfront, before work starts.
  • Orders of ₹10,000 and above: 50% advance to start, 50% before final files are released.
  • Rush delivery: add 30%.
  • Prices are final. No GST is charged.
  • Scope, deliverables, revisions and timeline are confirmed in writing before you pay.
