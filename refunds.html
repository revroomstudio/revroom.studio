Refund and cancellation policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

1. Before work starts

If you cancel before work on your project has started, we refund the advance you paid.

2. Once work has started

The advance is non-refundable once work has started. Work has started when we have begun drafting, designing or modelling against the written scope you confirmed.

3. If we cannot deliver

If we are unable to deliver the agreed scope, we refund the part of your payment that relates to work not delivered.

4. Revisions and changes

Revisions within the written scope are included in the fee. Changes outside the scope are quoted separately and are not part of any refund claim.

5. Duplicate or failed payments

If you were charged twice, or your payment failed but the amount was deducted, write to us with the payment reference and we will resolve it. Failed payments are normally returned automatically by your bank or Razorpay.

6. How refunds are made

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. They normally reach your account within 5 to 7 working days of approval, depending on your bank.

7. How to request a refund

Email us with your name, payment reference and reason for the request.
